# Onboarding Guide, Acme Robotics

## First week

Day 1 starts at 9:30 in the Prague office (Karlín, Rohanské nábřeží 19) with
badge pickup and laptop setup. Every new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy
for the first 6 weeks; the buddy is from a different team on purpose.

## The first month

Goals: ship one small change to production (engineering) or own one customer
interaction end to end (non-engineering). Managers hold weekly 1:1s during
the entire probation period (3 months).

## Tools to set up

Okta, Slack, Workday, Linear, and the internal wiki (Confluence). Engineers
additionally need the `acme-wg` VPN profile and access to the GitHub org,
requested in #it-requests.

## Probation review

A written review at the end of month 3 covers delivery, collaboration, and
values fit. The template lives in Workday; both sides fill it in before the
review meeting.
