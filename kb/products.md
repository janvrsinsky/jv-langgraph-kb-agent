# Product Overview, Acme Robotics

## AcmeArm 3000

Our flagship 6-axis industrial arm. Payload 12 kg, reach 1.4 m, maximum joint
torque 310 Nm, repeatability ±0.02 mm. Ships with the AcmeOS 4 controller and
a Python SDK. Typical customers: automotive tier-2 suppliers and electronics
assembly lines.

## AcmeCart AMR

Autonomous mobile robot for intralogistics. Payload 250 kg, runtime 9 hours,
swappable battery, LiDAR + visual SLAM navigation. Fleet coordination is
handled by the FleetMind server (up to 120 carts per site).

## FleetMind

Server software for orchestrating mixed fleets of AcmeArms and AcmeCarts.
Licensed per site, with a hard dependency on PostgreSQL 15+ and Kubernetes.
The 2026 roadmap adds an LLM-based natural-language task planner ("FleetMind
Copilot") currently in private beta with three customers.

## Support tiers

Standard (next business day), Gold (4-hour response, 99.5% uptime SLA on
FleetMind), Platinum (1-hour response, dedicated TAM). Robot firmware updates
are included in all tiers; on-site service only in Gold and above.
