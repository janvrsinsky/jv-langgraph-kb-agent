# Benefits, Acme Robotics

## Health

Private health cover for the employee from day one, with an option to add
family at cost. It includes a yearly preventive check and physiotherapy, which
is the benefit the assembly and service teams actually use most.

## Pension

Acme contributes 3 percent of salary to a supplementary pension scheme, rising
to 5 percent after three years of service. Employee contributions are matched
up to a further 2 percent.

## Meals

A meal allowance is paid as a monetary contribution, not vouchers, and appears
on the payslip. There is no canteen in the Karlín office; the allowance is set
against local prices and reviewed each January.

## Learning budget

25 000 CZK per person per year for courses, conferences and books, plus five
learning days that do not come out of vacation. Unused budget does not carry
over, which is deliberate: the point is that it gets used.

## Equipment beyond the laptop

Engineers choose between a MacBook Pro 14 inch and a ThinkPad X1 running
Ubuntu LTS, refreshed every 36 months. Peripherals come from the home office
allowance rather than from IT stock.

## Time off beyond vacation

Two paid volunteering days a year, and a paid day for moving house once every
two years. Sick leave is unlimited with a doctor's note from the fourth
consecutive day, which is covered in the HR policy rather than here.

## Referral bonus

50 000 CZK for a successful engineering referral, paid after the referred
person completes probation. There is no cap on the number of referrals and no
bonus for referring someone into a role you manage.
