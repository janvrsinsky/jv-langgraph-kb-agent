# HR Policies, Acme Robotics

## Vacation

Full-time employees receive 27 vacation days per year, accrued monthly.
Unused days roll over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year.
Vacation requests go through Workday and need manager approval at least
two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is unlimited but requires a doctor's note from the fourth
consecutive day. Notify your manager and log the absence in Workday on day one.

## Remote work

Acme operates a hybrid model: minimum two office days per week (Tuesday and
Thursday are anchor days). Fully remote weeks require director approval.
Working from abroad is capped at 30 days per year and must be cleared with
HR for tax reasons.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 18 weeks fully paid, secondary caregivers 6 weeks.
Leave can be split into two blocks within the child's first year.
