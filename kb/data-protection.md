# Data Protection, Acme Robotics

## What we hold

Employee records in Workday, customer contact details in the CRM, and robot
telemetry from customer sites. Telemetry is the sensitive one: it describes how
a customer's line runs, which is commercially valuable to them and to their
competitors.

## Telemetry ownership

Telemetry belongs to the customer. Acme processes it to operate and improve the
fleet under the service agreement. Using it for anything else, including
aggregate benchmarking published outside the company, needs the customer's
written consent, and consent for one purpose is not consent for another.

## Retention

Telemetry is retained for 24 months and then deleted. Support case data follows
the case, plus three years for warranty reasons. Employee records follow the
statutory retention period and nobody shortens or extends them locally.

## Subject requests

A request from an individual for their own data goes to
privacy@acme-robotics.example and is answered within one month. Do not answer
it directly even when the answer seems obvious, because the response has to be
complete and logged.

## Breach notification

A suspected personal data breach is reported to the privacy team within 24
hours of noticing it, which is deliberately shorter than the regulatory 72
hours so there is time to assess. Reporting is not an admission that a breach
occurred.

## Transfers outside the EU

Customer telemetry stays in EU regions. A support engineer travelling outside
the EU may access it for an active case but may not export it to local storage.
The distinction is where the data comes to rest, not where the person is.

## Anonymisation

Removing names is not anonymisation when the site and the timestamps remain,
because a fleet layout identifies a customer on its own. Anything leaving the
company as an example is rebuilt on synthetic data instead.
