# Expenses and Travel, Acme Robotics

## Booking

Flights and hotels are booked through the corporate travel tool. Anything over
30 000 CZK per trip needs director approval before booking, not after. Trains
under four hours are booked instead of flights on the same route as a default,
and the exception is a same-day return.

## Daily allowances

Domestic and international per diems follow the statutory Czech rates. Meals
paid by the company reduce the per diem for that day; this is automatic in the
tool and is not something to calculate by hand.

## What is reimbursed

Travel to a customer site, accommodation, per diem, visas, and reasonable
baggage. Laundry on trips over five nights. Alcohol is not reimbursed, and
neither are fines, upgrades bought at the airport, or a companion's costs.

## Receipts

Submit within 30 days with a photo of the receipt. Card statements are not
receipts. Anything without a receipt over 500 CZK is not reimbursable, and the
30-day limit exists because the accounting close is monthly.

## Customer entertainment

A meal with a customer is reimbursable with the names of the attendees on the
claim. Gifts of any value to a public-sector customer are not permitted at all,
and to a private-sector customer are capped at 1 000 CZK per person per year.

## Home office equipment

A one-off allowance of 15 000 CZK covers a chair, a desk and a monitor, claimed
the same way as any other expense. It renews every 36 months alongside the
laptop refresh.

## Mileage

Private car use is reimbursed per kilometre at the statutory rate, logged with
start and end points. It is only reimbursable when public transport or a train
would have been materially slower or is unavailable.
